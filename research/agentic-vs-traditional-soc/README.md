# Scaling with people vs. with agents

**Evaluation**

The core decision: cover growing alert volume by adding analysts (in-house or via a traditional human-led MDR), or by adding agents that do the first-line work.
The comparisons here measure the agentic model against a traditional SOC, an in-house build, MDR, MSSP, SIEM, and EDR. The aim is not to define each term; it is to show where cost, speed, coverage, and control actually differ.
