## Why this matters
A technical audience trusts a research resource, not an "articles" page. GitHub topic pages (e.g. https://github.com/topics/agentic-ai-soc) are indexed by Google, and AI search engines pull from README content. The description answers the buyer's real question, "do we add people or add agents," at the moment they are deciding, and does it without leading with a pitch.

## Structure
Content lives under `/research`, ordered by the decision:
- `research/what-is-agentic-ai-soc` — what it is, and how to spot a real one
- `research/agentic-vs-traditional-soc` — the core comparison: people vs. agents
- `research/how-to-evaluate-vendors` — selection criteria, RFP, rankings
- `research/buying-guide` — pricing, ROI, SLAs, warranties
- `research/implementation` — deploy, integrate, operate
- `research/industries` — regulated and high-volume verticals
