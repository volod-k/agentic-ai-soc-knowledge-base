# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in improving this knowledge base.

## What kinds of contributions we accept

- ✅ **Factual corrections** — a benchmark is wrong, a vendor's pricing has changed, a link is dead
- ✅ **New articles** — a topic in the Agentic AI SOC space we haven't covered yet
- ✅ **Clarifications** — a section that's ambiguous, an undefined term, a missing diagram
- ✅ **Translations** — translated versions of articles into other languages
- ✅ **Better examples** — real-world case studies, configuration snippets, vendor data points

## What we won't merge

- ❌ Marketing content for other vendors (we are vendor-aware, not vendor-promotional)
- ❌ Content that simply rephrases an existing article without adding new information
- ❌ Speculative or unverifiable claims about specific vendors

## How to submit

1. **Small fixes** — open a PR directly. Branch from `main`, edit the file, open the PR.
2. **New articles or major changes** — open an issue first to discuss scope before writing.

## Article style

- Front matter at the top (title, slug, category, tags) — copy the format from any existing article
- Use H2 (`##`) for major sections, H3 (`###`) for sub-sections
- Cite vendor pricing and benchmarks with source (Vendr, SelectHub, public price list, etc.)
- Prefer concrete numbers over generic ranges
- Plain Markdown only — no HTML unless absolutely necessary for tables
